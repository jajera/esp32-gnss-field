# Product

This repo is a hands-on GNSS field bring-up: wire a classic ESP32, ATGM336H, and ST7789 240×240, flash stage firmware, and write it up as a Zensical walkthrough.

We’re building a **portable GNSS field node** — fix quality + position on the TFT. Not NTRIP/RTK, not SeisComP, not SoftAP in v1, not a multi-board fleet.

Later extras (SoftAP, SD log, PPS clock, NTRIP client) land as new phase folders after v1.
